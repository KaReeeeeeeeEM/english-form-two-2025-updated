(e) Study the following text to enhance your skills in agreeing with others’ opinions politely.

Agreeing with others’ opinions

Respecting others’ opinions crucially shows that you value their knowledge

and contributions to the topic. When you respect others, it helps to build good relationships and encourages them to be positive. It is a way of showing that you

think they are important. It also shows the respect you expect them to reciprocate.
